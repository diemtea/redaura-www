How it works

01

You publish

Log in to your dashboard. Upload the test result, draw a boundary around the festival or city, and hit publish. Alerts are instantly formatted to international public health standards (CAP). The whole process takes exactly as long as it took to read this.

02

We deliver

Every phone inside that fence gets an instant push notification, credited to your organization. Even if someone is just visiting for the weekend, their phone already knows who to trust.

03

Nobody is tracked

Geofence matching happens locally on the user's device. No accounts, no location pings home, and no logs to subpoena. Privacy isn't a setting we offer. It's the architecture.

04

Native in every language

You publish in your language; the alert arrives in theirs. Translation happens on delivery, so a warning written in Dutch reads flawlessly in English, German, or Polish. No extra work for your team.

The demo app — live prototype

One app. Your name on every alert.

Users download a single global app. The data on it is yours: every alert carries your organization's name, your photo, your lab numbers. The trust you built on the ground travels with the warning.

Try it! Tap an alert to open it. A push lands a few seconds in, or replay it under the phone.
